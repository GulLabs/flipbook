# Governance

flipbook is maintained by [Gul Labs](https://github.com/gul-labs).

| Role       | Who                                        | Scope                                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Maintainer | [@atifgul99](https://github.com/atifgul99) | Product direction, reviews, releases, `main` |

Until additional maintainers are named:

- `@atifgul99` is the only person who can push to `main` or merge pull requests.
- Branch protection and repository rulesets enforce that restriction on GitHub.

There is no CLA. Contributions are accepted under the repository [MIT](./LICENSE) license.

To propose becoming a maintainer, open a discussion in a pull request that already shows sustained, high-quality work on the project. Do not open an issue titled “make me a maintainer.”
